# Software Defined Radios

- [ADALM-PLUTO (PlutoSDR)](adalm-pluto-plutosdr.md) — Active learning module (PlutoSDR) used to explore software-defined radio, RF experimentation, and wireless communications.
- [Airspy](airspy.md) — Receive-only SDRs with high dynamic range and front-end filtering, covering HF through 1.8 GHz depending on model.
- [bladeRF](bladerf.md) — Full-duplex 2x2 MIMO SDR tuning 47 MHz to 6 GHz, with an on-board FPGA for signal processing at the edge.
- [HackRF One](hackrf-one.md) — Software Defined Radio peripheral capable of transmission or reception of radio signals from 1 MHz to 6 GHz.
- [LimeSDR](limesdr.md) — Open-hardware full-duplex MIMO SDR with open drivers (LimeSuite, SoapySDR), widely used for LTE and 5G experimentation.
- [RTL-SDR](rtl-sdr.md) — Very cheap ~$30 USB dongle that can be used as a computer based radio scanner for receiving live radio signals in your area (no internet required).
- [SDRplay RSP](sdrplay-rsp.md) — Wideband 14-bit receivers covering 1 kHz to 2 GHz with tunable preselection filters to reject out-of-band interference.
- [USRP](usrp.md) — Ettus Research SDR family and the UHD driver stack; the platform most published cellular and wireless security research is built on.
