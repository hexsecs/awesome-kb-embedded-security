# Firmware Supply Chain and SBOM

- [CVE Binary Tool](cve-binary-tool.md) — OpenSSF tool that scans binaries directly for 350+ known-vulnerable open source components (OpenSSL, libpng, BusyBox, and more), without requiring a…
- [Grype](grype.md) — Vulnerability scanner that consumes SBOMs to identify known CVEs in firmware dependencies.
- [in-toto](in-toto.md) — Framework for supply chain integrity that records signed provenance steps and enforces layout verification.
- [Sigstore Cosign](sigstore-cosign.md) — Tooling for keyless signing and verification of firmware/container artifacts in CI/CD pipelines.
- [Syft](syft.md) — SBOM generator for filesystems and artifacts, useful for firmware package/component inventories.
